Automated Web Scraping and E-Commerce Price Monitoring System

Abdullah Adnan
Department of Computer Science
Air University, Islamabad
abdullah@example.com

Abstract—This paper presents an automated web scraping framework designed for real-time lead generation and product price monitoring across e-commerce platforms such as Amazon and eBay. The system extracts targeted metadata, filters contact details, and tracks price variations to trigger automated SMTP notifications upon reaching predefined target thresholds.

Index Terms—Web Scraping, Automation, Python, Price Tracking, Lead Generation, Information Extraction.

I. Introduction

The rapid expansion of e-commerce platforms has created a vast ecosystem of dynamically changing product data. Manual tracking of price fluctuations across multiple vendors is computationally inefficient and time-consuming.

In this work, we propose a modular Python-based automation tool that periodically scrapes product details, stores history, and alerts users when targeted discount thresholds are met.

II. Related Work

Data extraction techniques relying on Document Object Model (DOM) parsing have evolved significantly. Traditional regex parsers are often brittle against modern client-side rendering frameworks.
